What needs verification

The layout is traced and scaled from the PDF plans. The external rear spiral stair is represented schematically; use the floor controls or internal stairs for movement between levels. Small offsets, openings, façade details and stair treads are approximate. Stair openings and walls under the roof stair are simplified to fit the assumed flights; stair headroom must be checked from detailed sections. The source drawings take precedence over this model's measurements.

Furniture, interior materials and lighting are illustrative. Sunlight is for visibility; this is not a daylight study. Doors are shown open for circulation. Furniture is visual only; movement does not collide with it. Lamp placement and brightness illustrate a possible interior mood, not an electrical or lighting design.

Structural PDF: 29 pages, marked NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION. Reinforcement, foundations, beam drops, services and structural adequacy are not evaluated or fully modeled.

Useful places to review

  • Entrance foyer: sightlines into the lounge and drawing/dining room.
  • Angled stairs and first-floor bridge: circulation, railing design and headroom.
  • Open kitchen: working space around the island and access to the dirty kitchen.
  • Upper terraces and roof: access, privacy and parapet details.
